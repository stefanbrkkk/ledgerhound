Security

Accountants trust us with their client relationships. Here is exactly what we store, where, and who can touch it. The short version: v1 stores the checklist, not the documents.

What we store (v1)

  • The extracted document checklist: client name, document type, received/missing status, dates.
  • Your firm's account details and the nudge drafts you generate.
  • We do not store your clients' forwarded email bodies or the documents themselves beyond what is needed to produce the checklist, and we never use your data to train AI models.

Where it lives

  • Hosting: [HOSTING PROVIDER], EU region (e.g. Hetzner Nuremberg / Supabase EU).
  • Encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+, HSTS) and at rest; strict content-security policy on this site.
  • Payments: handled entirely by our merchant of record; we never see card details.

Who can access it

  • The founder (operator) and named sub-processors only: hosting provider, payment MoR, and AI model provider under their data-processing agreements (OpenAI/Anthropic DPAs; business data is not used for model training by default).
  • Current sub-processor list with DPA links is available on request and in the customer DPA.

Your controls

  • One-click export of everything, anytime.
  • One-click deletion of a client record — or your whole account.
  • A signed Data Processing Agreement (GDPR Art. 28 shape) for firm customers on request.

Reporting a vulnerability

Found something? Email hello@ledgerhound.app with details. We respond within 2 business days and credit responsible disclosures (if you want).
