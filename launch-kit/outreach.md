## 2. The DM/email scripts (Mom-Test compliant — past behavior + money only)

### 2a. Cold-but-warm DM/email (US/UK firms)
Subject: chasing client docs (30-sec question)

Hi [name] — quick one, bookkeeper to bookkeeper.

Over the last 30 days, roughly how many hours did you or your team spend chasing clients for missing documents — statements, receipts, payroll confirmations? And what are you using today for that: a portal, plain email, a VA?

Asking because I'm building a small tool that turns forwarded client emails into a "still missing from X/Y/Z" chase list every Monday, with ready-to-send nudges. You'd forward the mess, it does the diff, you send.

I'm taking 20 founding firms: $349 for the first year, billed once, fully refundable until v1 ships. If it sounds useful, reply "founding" and I'll send the checkout. If not — did the hours number surprise you?

[Your name]

### 2b. Follow-up (day 3, if silence)
One-line bump: "Did you see this — worth 2 minutes, or totally off-base for your firm?"

### 2c. Reply handling
- "How much time?": share the math (3 hrs/wk × $150 × 48 = $21,600/yr billed value).
- "Is my data safe?": forward-in only, checklist-only storage, EU hosting, DPA on request.
- "Send checkout": Polar link (see 4).
