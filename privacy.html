Privacy Policy

Ledgerhound is a document-chasing tool for bookkeeping and accounting firms. This policy covers this website and, at a high level, how the product treats data. The plain-English summary: we collect almost nothing, we store the least we can, and you can delete what exists.

1. Who we are

The data controller for this website is [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], [REGISTERED ADDRESS], Belgrade, Serbia (m.b. [MB], PIB [PIB]). Contact: hello@ledgerhound.app. Serbia is outside the EU/EEA; where EU/UK data protection law applies to us, we comply with it directly as set out below.

2. What this website collects

  • Founding-spot and demo-unlock requests. If you submit the request form or unlock the demo, we store the email address you give us (and firm name, if you provide it) for one purpose only: sending you your founding invite or demo unlock. We do not add you to a newsletter.
  • The interactive demo. It runs entirely in your browser. The sample emails and checklist never leave your device; your run counter is stored in your browser's local storage and you can clear it anytime via the demo's reset or by clearing site data.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider keeps standard, short-lived request logs for security and abuse prevention (IP address, timestamp, requested URL). We do not use them for profiling.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels, no third-party cookies. This site sets no marketing or analytics cookies, so you will not see a cookie banner. If we ever add analytics, this policy and a consent banner will come first.

3. What the product stores (for customers)

Once you become a customer, Ledgerhound v1 stores the extracted document checklist — client name, document type, received/missing status, and dates — plus your firm's account details and the nudge drafts you generate. V1 does not store your clients' email bodies or the documents themselves beyond what is needed to produce the checklist, and it never contacts your clients by itself. A signed Data Processing Agreement (with an EU sub-processor list and EU-region hosting) is available on request for firm customers.

4. Legal bases (where GDPR / UK GDPR applies)

  • Requests you send us (founding form, demo unlock): performance of a contract or steps prior to entering a contract — Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • Security logs: our legitimate interest in operating a safe service — Art. 6(1)(f).
  • Product data: performance of the customer contract — Art. 6(1)(b).

5. Your rights

Where GDPR or UK GDPR applies, you can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability of your personal data, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Email hello@ledgerhound.app and we will respond within 30 days. If you are in the EU/EEA and believe we mishandled your data, you can complain to your national data protection authority; if you are in the UK, to the ICO. Serbian residents may also contact the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection.

6. Processors we use

  • Merchant of record ([MERCHANT OF RECORD LEGAL NAME]) — payment processing and tax compliance for paid plans. When you pay, they act as the seller of record and process payment data under their own privacy policy.
  • EU-region hosting — website and product data hosting.
  • AI model providers (used by the product, not this site) — document-checklist extraction, under data-processing agreements with no training on your data. Current list available on request and in the product DPA.

7. Retention

Founding/waitlist requests: kept until the cohort is filled or you ask us to delete them, whichever comes first. Product checklists: kept while your subscription is active and deleted within 30 days of cancellation or on your one-click export-and-delete request. Security logs: 30 days or less.

8. California & other US state laws

We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not process enough data for California's CCPA/CPRA thresholds to apply at this stage. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated first.

9. Changes

If we change this policy materially, we will update the date at the top and, for product customers, notify you by email before the change takes effect.
