— 02 · How it works

A Monday cadence, not another portal.

No client logins to chase, no inbox connection required at launch. The whole loop runs on things you already have: your email, your checklist, your send button.

  1. FWD · MON 09:00

    Forward the mess

    Dump last week's client emails into your Ledgerhound address — the half-sentences, the "see attached", the scanned piles. Paste last year's checklist once per client; it remembers.

    It works out which documents actually arrived, from whom, and what's still outstanding against your checklist — with confidence flags on anything uncertain.

  3. CHASE · MON 09:15

    A per-client chase list with ready-to-send nudge drafts in your tone. Read, tweak, send. Nothing leaves without your finger on the button — ever.

        — 05 · FAQ

        Asked by every firm so far.

        What exactly does Ledgerhound do?

        Once a week you forward your clients' document emails to a dedicated Ledgerhound address. It reads what actually arrived, matches it against each client's checklist (seeded from last year), and produces a chase list: who owes what, with confidence flags. It drafts the nudge emails. You review and send them. It never emails anyone by itself.

        Do you connect to or read our inbox?

        Not at launch. V1 runs on forwarding — no OAuth, no inbox scopes, nothing to break. A read-only inbox integration is on the roadmap and founding firms decide if and when it ships; it would be strictly opt-in and read-only.

        Is our client data safe?

        V1 stores the extracted document checklist — client name, document type, received/missing, dates — not your clients' documents or full email bodies. Hosting is in the EU, encrypted in transit; a signed DPA with a sub-processor list is available on request, and you can delete a client's record anytime. The demo on this page runs entirely in your browser and sends nothing anywhere.

        Who sends the chase emails?

        You do. Ledgerhound drafts; you read, edit, and send from your own mailbox. This is deliberate: your clients hear from you, deliverability stays yours, and nothing embarrassing goes out on autopilot.

        Is this accounting or legal advice?

        No. Ledgerhound is a drafting and tracking tool. Every output is a starting point for a professional — you — to review. That's a feature, not a hedge.

        What if it misses something?

        Uncertain matches carry confidence flags instead of silent guesses, and founding firms get a weekly human hand-check of every generated list during onboarding. The diff is against your checklist — the hound only claims what it can point at.

        How does the refund work?

        Founding plans are refundable in full, no questions asked, until v1 ships to the founding cohort. After that, cancel in one click anytime; the price stays locked while you're subscribed, and your data exports in one click too. Full wording is on the refunds page.

        Where do the numbers on this page come from?

        The chasing-hours range is a verbatim quote from an active r/Accounting thread (Aug 2026); the $21,600/year figure is published firm arithmetic (3 hrs/wk × $150 × 48 weeks) we verified; the wage and firm counts are US BLS (May 2025) and industry counts (Jan 2025). The hero ticker multiplies ~52,200 US CPA firms by a conservative 5 hrs/week — it's an estimate, and we label it as one. Full sources ship with our research notes on request.
